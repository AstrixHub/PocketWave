# **PocketWave 1.0.3!**

## Updates

- Make playlists! Open the GUI with `/music playlist` (or the new Open Playlists keybind)
- Create playlists, click into them, and add songs by name, URL, or whatever’s playing right now
- Loop a whole playlist so it keeps going after the last song
- Jump straight in with `/music playlist Gaming Playlist` (use your playlist’s name)
- Playlists save automatically and come back next time you launch
- Reorder songs with Move Up and Move Down while editing a playlist
- The next song is ready early, so playlists and queues feel smoother
- Open `/music settings` to pick your default volume (the next song starts at that level), how much volume up/down changes, and whether the now-playing HUD shows
