# **PocketWave 1.2.0! (Untested)**

## Updates

- PocketWave now works on Minecraft 26.3 (Fabric Loader 0.20.1+)
- Everything from the 1.0.x versions is here — playlists, settings, queue, HUD, the works
- Asks before downloading ffmpeg (and you can turn that permission on/off in `/music settings`)
- Queue only preloads the next couple of songs (same idea as playlists), so filling the queue doesn’t hammer your disk/CPU
- Checks SHA-256 on bundled yt-dlp and downloaded ffmpeg before running them
- Only allows http/https links for playback (no odd schemes), and passes them to yt-dlp more safely

# **PocketWave 1.0.4!**

## Updates

- First-time setup guide (like Simple Voice Chat) explains what PocketWave does and how to use it — reopen anytime from `/music settings` → Setup guide
- Asks before downloading ffmpeg (small helper, about 20–30 MB) and you can turn that permission on/off in `/music settings`
- Searching a song is less likely to grab a random video that doesn’t match what you typed
- Runs a bit lighter while music is playing (less extra memory, and the HUD does less work)
- `/music playlist` now suggests your playlist names while you type

# **PocketWave 1.0.3!**

## Updates

- Make playlists! Open the GUI with `/music playlist` (or the new Open Playlists keybind)
- Create playlists, click into them, and add songs by name, URL, or whatever’s playing right now
- Loop a whole playlist so it keeps going after the last song
- Jump straight in with `/music playlist Gaming Playlist` (use your playlist’s name)
- Playlists save automatically and come back next time you launch
- Reorder songs with Move Up and Move Down while editing a playlist
- The next song is ready early, so playlists and queues feel smoother
- Open `/music settings` to pick your default volume (the next song starts at that level), how much volume up/down changes, and whether the now-playing HUD shows
