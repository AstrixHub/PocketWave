# **PocketWave 1.0.2!**

## Updates

- Queue songs with `/music queue` so the next one plays automatically
- Check what’s next with `/music queue`, or clear it with `/music queue clear`
- New **Show Queue** keybind (unbound by default) that prints the queue in chat
- The next queued song starts right away, instead of sitting through a loading pause
- Long song names get shortened so they don’t take over the screen
- The volume bar looks like the vanilla XP bar
- The widget hides when a song ends (unless you’re looping)
- Shows when a song is paused, looping, or if more songs are queued
- ffmpeg only needs to download once
