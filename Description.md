# **What PocketWave is**

PocketWave is a Minecraft mod created to allow players to play music from YouTube, YouTube Music, and other supported sites*, anywhere, anytime!

## **Features:**

PocketWave has one simple feature: Allow the user to play music from YouTube or YouTube Music whenever they want.

### Commands:

> /music play <url/search term> | Find and play the audio track
> 
> /music local <path> | Play a local audio file (For offline playback)
> 
> /music pause | Pause (/music resume also works)
> 
> /music stop | Stops audio playback
>
> /music volup | Increases volume (/music voldown also works) by 10%
>
> /music volume <0-100> | Set volume
>
> /music now | Shows current track/status
>
> /music loop | Toggles looping of the current song

### Keybinds:
Unbound by default

- Pause/Resume
- Volume Up
- Volume Down
- Stop
- Toggle Loop

### HUD:

While a track is playing or paused (Or loading), a small widget appears in the **top-left** - Song title and a thin volume bar. Stays out of the crosshair/hotbar area and hides with the rest of the HUD (F1).

## **Troubleshooting**
Explained by AI, since I don't understand

**“YouTube blocked this request…”**  
YouTube’s bot check rejected the download. Try again later, or use another link / a local file. This mod does not require Google login.

**ffmpeg download failed**  
Allow Minecraft network access, or install ffmpeg yourself (`brew install ffmpeg` on macOS) and restart the game.

**Spotify links**  
Not supported. Use YouTube, YouTube Music, SoundCloud, Bandcamp, direct audio URLs, or `/music local`.

*Sites supported by PocketWave are any sites that support yt-dlp, (https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp).
